## 0. Version marker — THE BUILD IDENTIFIES ITSELF AS R4, NOT R5

The title screen (华山论剑) carries **no** round marker anywhere. I magnified all four
corners and the footer of the title canvas: the only footer text is `没有找到存档`.

The marker lives on the **Settings** screen (设置). It reads, verbatim:

    版本 R4 · 2026-09-03

**It does not say R5.** By the rule I was given, everything below is therefore a report
about a build that identifies itself as R4.

Complicating fact, reported without interpretation: the HTTP `last-modified` of the
deployed index.html is 2026-09-04 19:07 GMT (today, ~8 minutes before I loaded it), and
several things R5 claimed *are* present in the build (the no-trainable-gongfa guard,
the pause menu with two-press confirm, consequence text on most pick screens). So the
deployed bytes are fresh; the version string is either stale or was never bumped. I
cannot tell from the client which. Treat every verdict below as "verdict against the
build labelled R4 that is live right now".

### Measurement caveat that affects claim 5 (read before trusting any timing)
The harness's browser pane reports `document.visibilityState === "hidden"`, which makes
Chrome throttle `requestAnimationFrame` to ~0.25 fps. Under that throttle I measured
enemy turns of **15–40 s** — those numbers are artefacts, not the game. I replaced
`window.requestAnimationFrame` with a `setTimeout(…,16)` shim, verified the game's own
main loop then ran at **60.8–61.9 fps** (counted by instrumenting the rAF the engine
calls), and re-took every timing. **All timings in section 5 are post-fix, at ~61 fps.**

A second instrument note: keyboard events synthesised by the pane's `key` action arrive
with an empty `code` and `keyCode 0`, and Godot ignores most of them. Early "Escape does
nothing" readings were instrument error. I re-ran every keyboard test with well-formed
`KeyboardEvent`s (`key`/`code`/`keyCode` all set) dispatched at the canvas, and **every
Escape/Space result below is from the well-formed events**.

### Route through the game
新的冒险 → attribute allocation → trait pick → confirm → **prologue battle 华山论剑**
(you are 独臂大虾 vs five masters) → win → 拜入门派 → monthly loop (机缘 → 本月行动 →
练功/修习/做工/游历/存档/读档/回档) → 年关抉择 → next year. I won the prologue twice and
played through 第1年1月 to 第2年2月.

## 2. Back on every uncommitted screen — **PARTIAL, and Escape is dangerous**

Back buttons exist widely. Escape is inconsistent across four different behaviours, and
on one screen it destroys the run.

| Screen | Visible back | Escape (well-formed event) | State after back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attribute allocation | 返回菜单 | **no-op** | — |
| Trait pick | 上一步 / 下一步 | **no-op** | 上一步 from confirm kept 剩余 19 + 骨骼清奇 已选 |
| Creation confirm | 返回 | **no-op** | returns to trait screen unchanged |
| Battle | 退回 (undo move; greyed until you move) | opens 暂停 | — |
| 暂停 menu | 继续 | **does not close it** — Escape again, still open | — |
| **拜入门派 (sect select)** | 返回主菜单 | **jumps straight to the main menu, no confirmation** | **entire run lost** |
| 每月机缘 | 返回 | **skips the 机缘 and lands on 本月行动** | reward silently forfeited |
| 本月行动 | none | no-op | — |
| 练功 gongfa pick | 返回 | (not separately tested) | 第1年1月, 银两 0, 根骨 11 — unchanged |
| 修习 attribute pick | 返回 | (not separately tested) | unchanged |
| 游历 event | **none** | (not tested — would have consumed the run) | — |
| 【开年际遇】 | **none** | no-op | — |
| 年关抉择 | 返回 | acts as back to 本月行动 | see the exploit below |
| Defeat screen | only 重试 | no-op | — |

Three of these are defects, not gaps:

1. **Escape on 拜入门派 wipes the run.** I pressed Escape once on the sect screen and was
   on the main menu the next frame. No confirm, no "are you sure". The tournament I had
   just won was gone, and 读取存档 was still greyed with `没有找到存档` — there is no save
   before the jianghu begins, so the only recovery is to replay the whole prologue battle.
   I did replay it, which is how the rest of this report exists.
2. **Escape on 每月机缘 forfeits the reward.** At 第2年2月 the offered 机缘 was
   `一锭元宝(钱财)· 银两 +100`. Escape → 本月行动, silver **755 before and 755 after**,
   month unchanged. The +100 is simply gone, with no message.
3. **返回 on 年关抉择 lets you re-run the month, unboundedly.** At 第1年12月: 做工 →
   silver 653, year-end screen appears → press 返回 → back on 本月行动 **for the same
   第1年12月**, with `做工(本月 银两 +31)` offered again → 做工 → silver **684**, year-end
   again. Repeat: 684 → 718 (+34) → 755 (+37). The month never advances and the money is
   free. Escape from the year-end screen does the same thing.

Where back *does* work it works properly: 练功 → 返回 left 第1年·第1月, 银两 0, 根骨 11
byte-for-byte identical, and 上一步 in creation preserved both the point pool and the
selected trait.

**Documented-but-wrong keybinds.** The in-battle tutorial states `按 Esc 暂停/继续游戏。`
Escape opens the pause menu but does **not** close it — I sent three well-formed Escapes
in a row and the menu stayed open each time; only clicking 继续 resumes. The tutorial also
states `按空格结束回合`, and Space **does** work.

## 5. Battle feedback and pacing — **MET on pacing (arguably overshot), MET on feedback**

**Whose turn.** Top banner reads `行动: 独臂大虾 · 移动 4 · · · · · 行动 ✓` and updates to
`行动: 中神通虾 · …`, `行动: 南帝虾 · …` etc. A second line gives the full order:
`顺序: 独臂大虾 · 东邪虾 · 中神通虾 · 南帝虾 · 北丐虾 · 西毒虾`, and it re-orders between
rounds (round 2 put the player last) and drops the dead (after 东邪虾 died the line read
`顺序: 独臂大虾 · 中神通虾 · 南帝虾 · 北丐虾`). The acting unit wears a cyan ring; the
player's reachable tiles are tinted green.

**Timing — measured at a verified 61.8 fps** by sampling the acting-unit banner region of
the canvas every 200 ms and recording every change, with `performance.now()` timestamps:

- Space (end turn) pressed at **t = 0.14 s**.
- Banner region changed at **0.24, 0.34, 0.45, 0.81, 0.85, 1.44 s**. No change after 1.44 s.
- Screenshot at t = 6.15 s: **回合 3, 行动: 独臂大虾** — my turn again.

So the five-enemy round from end-turn to my next turn took **≈1.3 s**; the longest single
gap inside it was **0.59 s** (0.45 → 0.81). An independent run gave the same shape:
click 结束回合, last banner change at 1.46 s, player acting again well before t = 3.04 s.

Against the targets: **each enemy turn ≈0.1–0.6 s (target <2 s) and the enemy round ≈1.3 s
(target <10 s)** — met with a large margin. **The screen never sat still for more than a
second with nothing happening** during an enemy round.

The honest counterpoint: at 1.3 s for five enemies you cannot actually watch the enemy
round. Between my last input and my next turn the board simply rearranges. The combat log
is the only record of what happened, and it is the part of the screen that is hardest to
read (see Q6).

**Damage numbers and log.** Floating numbers appear over targets (`23`, `-35`, `-31`).
The lower-left log is real and per-event, e.g.
`中神通虾 → 独臂大虾 -35 (剩 942)` / `南帝虾 → 独臂大虾 -31 (剩 911)` /
`东邪虾 → 独臂大虾 -29 (剩 882)` / `独臂大虾 → 南帝虾 -100 (剩 0)`.
Failure feedback exists too: `射程不够` and `本回合已行动` print near the banner.

  (a) the combat log is painted in the lower-left **on top of skill buttons 1 and 2**, so

  overlap each other and neither is legible. Nothing is truncated with "...".

## Would make a player quit — worst first

1. **Escape on the sect-selection screen throws the run away with no confirmation and no
   save to fall back on.** You have just fought a ~15-round battle; one keypress on the very
   next screen returns you to the title, and 读取存档 is greyed with `没有找到存档` because
   the first save slot only becomes reachable *after* sect selection. This cost me a full
   playthrough and I had to redo the whole prologue.
2. **The prologue can be lost with no way out but 重试.** On `战败于华山论剑` the only
   control is 重试; 暂停 is inert and Escape does nothing. You cannot reach the main menu,
   the settings, or a save from a defeat.
3. **返回/Escape on the year-end screen re-runs the month forever** (653 → 684 → 718 → 755
   silver in one 第1年12月). Anyone who presses back once discovers the game has no economy.
4. **Escape on the monthly 机缘 silently burns the reward** — a `银两 +100` vanished with no
   message and no way back.
5. **The event screen has no exit and no information.** 游历 hands you `付钱渡河` /
   `泅水而过` with no stated cost or risk, no back button, and prints no outcome; you infer
   that you paid 10 silver by watching the stat line change.
6. **The combat log sits on top of the skill bar.** The one artefact that explains a
   1.3-second enemy round is overlaid on the buttons you need to read.
7. **Character creation is decorative for the first hour.** 30 points and a trait, then a
   prologue that hands you a fixed 1000 HP / 180 内力 body; and the game lets you walk past
   the allocation screen with all 30 points unspent without a word.
8. **`按 Esc 暂停/继续游戏` is wrong** — Escape pauses but will not unpause.
9. **存档 gives no confirmation.** Selecting it produces no message, no timestamp, no slot
   display; `localStorage` is empty (a Godot build would use IndexedDB, so this is not
